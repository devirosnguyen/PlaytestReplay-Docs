First release: recorder (streaming, crash-safe format, tracked objects, gaze, frame times, scenes, consent), replay window
(multi-session overlay, timeline, follow camera, view frustum), heatmaps window (position, volume, gaze, events,
performance; filters; PNG/CSV export), tester tools (bug key with screenshot and note, zip export, uploader, overlay),
samples (first person, third person, 12 recorded sessions).
